How to read this twin

A 3D model of Idukki dam in Kerala, driven by the real record of the October 2021 storm. It advises; it never operates a real gate.

  1. Now — the reservoir as the recording plays back. The badge top-right says REPLAY: this is 2021, not live.
  2. Try it — watch the storm hour by hour as the operators ran it and as AquaSync would have, or hold a release of your own for 72 hours.
  3. Tide & rig — the sea at the river mouth, and the bench model with its real sensor and gate.
What the words and badges mean
FRL
Full reservoir level, 732.43 m at Idukki. The highest it is meant to hold in normal operation.
Cushion
How far the water is below FRL: room left for the next storm. Bigger is safer.
Rule level
Where the operating rules want the reservoir at this time of year.
m MSL
Metres above mean sea level.
cumecs
Cubic metres of water per second.
Mm³
Millions of cubic metres.
LIVE
Measured now, by the rig. The only green badge.
REPLAY
A recorded episode being played back.
SIMULATED
The simulation view is driving the 3D model.
HINDSIGHT
The optimiser was shown the rain that actually came. Best case, not a forecast.
BUNDLED
A saved result, used because the server is not reachable.
STRESS TEST
The recorded storm scaled up. Not a forecast, not a return period.
PREDICTED
A model output, such as the tide.
OFFLINE
Needs the server, which is not running.

In the simulation: Space plays, ← → steps an hour, Shift ← → a day, Esc closes. Open ?sim=1&demo=1 on an unattended screen and it plays on a loop.
